Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 14, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Helm ("we," "our," or "us"). We take your privacy seriously and are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains, in clearly separated sections, exactly what data we collect, how we use it, how and where we store it, and how (and with whom) we share it, when you use the Helm Chrome Extension and its associated website (collectively, the "Service").

If you have any questions or concerns about this policy, please contact us at clavinovacs@gmail.com.

2. Data We Collect

We collect only the categories of data described below, and only to operate the Service. Each category is disclosed here and matches the data-collection disclosures declared on our Chrome Web Store listing.

2.1 Personally Identifiable Information (PII)

We collect your email address when you explicitly create an account via Google Authentication. This is the only personally identifiable information we collect.

2.2 Financial and Payment Information

We collect your purchase status and license type (e.g., whether you hold a Helm Pro license). Helm never sees, handles, or stores your credit card numbers or full billing information — that is handled entirely by our third-party payment processor, and Helm receives only a license-validation token.

2.3 Authentication Information

We collect Google OAuth tokens (managed by Firebase Authentication). If you choose to enter API keys for third-party AI services (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic), those keys are also collected and held locally on your device.

2.4 Personal Communications (Chat Messages)

To provide its core organizing features, Helm reads the content of your AI chat conversations from supported provider pages. This is the "personal communications" category we collect.

2.5 Website Content

Helm reads conversation metadata from supported AI provider websites (e.g., ChatGPT, Claude.ai, Gemini) — including conversation titles, timestamps, and provider names — in order to populate your workspace.

3. How We Use Your Data (Handling)

We use each category of collected data strictly for the purpose described below, and for no unrelated purpose:

  • Email / PII: to manage your account identity, verify your Helm Pro license status, and send transactional messages such as purchase confirmations.
  • Financial / payment status: to validate and unlock the features included with your license.
  • Authentication information: OAuth tokens are used to sign you in; your AI provider API keys are used solely to send your requests directly to that provider on your behalf.
  • Personal communications (chat content): processed locally within your browser to organize conversations into folders, display titles, and build your workspace.
  • Website content (metadata): used to list and organize your conversations inside the Helm dashboard.

We do not use any of this data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to Helm's single purpose, and we never use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.

4. How and Where We Store Your Data (Storage)

Different categories of data are stored in different places, as follows:

Data Where it is stored
Email / account identity Google Firebase (cloud)
License / Pro status Google Firebase (cloud)
Folder structure & conversation metadata Google Firebase (cloud), only if cloud sync is enabled
API keys, preferences, workspace data Your browser only, via chrome.storage.local — never sent to our servers
Chat message body text Temporary browser memory during your active session only — never uploaded to or stored on our servers

In short: the body text of your conversations is processed locally and is never transmitted to or stored on Helm's servers. Only the data shown above as "cloud" is stored remotely, and only on Google Firebase.

5. How We Share Your Data (Sharing)

We do not sell, rent, or transfer your personal data to data brokers or advertising networks. The only third parties that ever receive data are the subprocessors below, and only to operate the Service:

Subprocessor Data shared & purpose
Firebase (Google Cloud) Email and folder/conversation metadata — for authentication and optional cloud sync
RevenueCat License status — for subscription and entitlement management
Lemon Squeezy Purchase information — for payment processing and license issuance

We never share the body text of your chat conversations with any third party.

6. Third-Party AI Services

Helm integrates with third-party AI providers (such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and others) via your own API keys. When you use these integrations, your requests are sent directly from your browser to the respective AI provider's servers. We do not proxy, log, or intercept these API calls. Each provider's own privacy policy governs the data you send to them.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

You may permanently delete your account and all associated cloud data at any time from the Settings dashboard within the extension. Cloud-stored data (such as folder metadata and account information) is removed within 30 days of account deletion. All locally stored data (preferences, API keys, workspace data) is deleted immediately when you uninstall the Helm extension from Chrome.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

GDPR (EEA / UK Users)

  • Right of Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate personal data.
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Right to Restriction: Request that we restrict processing of your personal data.
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a portable format.
  • Right to Object: Object to our processing of your personal data.

CCPA (California Users)

  • Right to know what personal information we collect and how it is used.
  • Right to delete personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to opt out of the sale of your personal information. We do not sell personal information.
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at clavinovacs@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Children's Privacy

Helm is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect your information. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. We encourage you to use strong, unique API keys and to keep them confidential.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Your continued use of Helm after changes become effective constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
