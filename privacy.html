Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 20, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Helm ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have in relation to it when you use the Helm Chrome Extension and its associated website (collectively, the "Service").

If you have any questions or concerns about this policy, please contact us at clavinovacs@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • API keys you enter for third-party AI services (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic). These are stored locally on your device using chrome.storage.local and are never transmitted to our servers.
  • License keys issued by our payment processor (Lemon Squeezy) for verifying Pro subscription status.
  • Support communications you send to our email address.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Workspace preferences (theme, layout, accent color, card style, view density) stored locally via chrome.storage.local.
  • Folder and chat metadata you create within Helm (titles, ordering, timestamps) stored locally on your device.

2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • The content of your AI conversations. Helm does not read, store, or transmit the substance of chats you have with any AI provider.
  • Browsing history beyond what is necessary to display AI provider tabs you have open.
  • Personally identifiable information unless you voluntarily provide it (e.g., when contacting support).

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect or receive to:

  • Operate and maintain the Helm extension and its features.
  • Verify Pro subscription status by communicating with Lemon Squeezy's license validation API.
  • Respond to your support requests and inquiries.
  • Improve, personalize, and expand our Service.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Payment Processing & Lemon Squeezy

Helm uses Lemon Squeezy as its Merchant of Record for all payment processing. When you purchase a Pro subscription or Lifetime Access, you are transacting directly with Lemon Squeezy. Helm does not collect, store, or have access to your payment card details or billing information.

Lemon Squeezy's privacy practices are governed by their own privacy policy, available at lemonsqueezy.com/privacy. As Merchant of Record, Lemon Squeezy assumes responsibility for the lawful processing of payment transactions, including applicable tax obligations.

Purchases are completed inside the Helm extension itself, not on this website.

5. Third-Party AI Services

Helm integrates with third-party AI providers (such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and others) via your own API keys. When you use these integrations, your requests are sent directly from your browser to the respective AI provider's servers. We do not proxy, log, or intercept these API calls. Each provider's own privacy policy governs the data you send to them.

6. Local Storage & Data Retention

All workspace data (preferences, folders, chat metadata, API keys) is stored exclusively in your browser's local storage via the Chrome Extension Storage API (chrome.storage.local). This data resides on your device and is not synchronized to any Helm-operated server unless you explicitly enable a cloud backup feature (if offered in a future release).

You can delete all locally stored data at any time by uninstalling the Helm extension from Chrome.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

GDPR (EEA / UK Users)

  • Right of Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate personal data.
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Right to Restriction: Request that we restrict processing of your personal data.
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a portable format.
  • Right to Object: Object to our processing of your personal data.

CCPA (California Users)

  • Right to know what personal information we collect and how it is used.
  • Right to delete personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to opt out of the sale of your personal information. We do not sell personal information.
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at clavinovacs@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

Helm is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect your information. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. We encourage you to use strong, unique API keys and to keep them confidential.
