Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 11, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Helm ("we," "our," or "us"). We take your privacy seriously and are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we store it, and what rights you have in relation to it when you use the Helm Chrome Extension and its associated website (collectively, the "Service").

If you have any questions or concerns about this policy, please contact us at clavinovacs@gmail.com.

2. Data We Collect and How We Use It

2.1 Personally Identifiable Information (PII)

We collect email addresses when users explicitly create accounts via Google Authentication. This data is used to manage account identity, verify Helm Pro license status, and send transactional communications (such as purchase confirmations). Storage occurs on Google Firebase. This information is never sold or shared with advertisers.

2.2 Financial and Payment Information

Purchase status and license type are collected for subscription validation purposes. Helm never directly sees, handles, or stores your credit card numbers or full billing information. Payment processing is handled entirely by our third-party payment processors, and Helm receives only license validation tokens. License status is stored on Firebase; full transaction records remain with the payment processors.

2.3 Authentication Information

We collect Google OAuth tokens, which are managed by Firebase Authentication. If you choose to enter API keys for third-party AI services (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic), those keys are encrypted and stored entirely within your browser's local storage via chrome.storage.local — they are never transmitted to our servers.

2.4 Personal Communications (Chat Messages)

Helm reads the content of AI chat conversations locally within your browser in order to enable core features such as organizing conversations into folders, displaying titles, and building your workspace. We do not upload, transmit, or store the body text of your conversations on our servers. This content exists only in temporary browser memory during your active session and is never shared with third parties.

2.5 Website Content

The extension accesses supported AI provider websites (e.g., ChatGPT, Claude.ai, Gemini) to read conversation metadata — including titles, timestamps, and provider names — in order to populate your Helm workspace. Only metadata syncs to the cloud if you explicitly enable that feature; raw conversation content is never transmitted to our servers.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

You may permanently delete your account and all associated cloud data at any time from the Settings dashboard within the extension. Cloud-stored data (such as folder metadata and account information) is removed within 30 days of account deletion. All locally stored data (preferences, API keys, workspace data) is deleted immediately when you uninstall the Helm extension from Chrome.

4. Data Sharing — Third-Party Subprocessors

We do not sell, rent, or transfer your personal data to any third parties, data brokers, or advertising networks. The only third parties that receive data do so strictly to operate the Service:

Subprocessor Purpose
Firebase (Google Cloud) User authentication, cloud sync of folder metadata
RevenueCat License and subscription management
Stripe Payment processing

5. Third-Party AI Services

Helm integrates with third-party AI providers (such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and others) via your own API keys. When you use these integrations, your requests are sent directly from your browser to the respective AI provider's servers. We do not proxy, log, or intercept these API calls. Each provider's own privacy policy governs the data you send to them.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

GDPR (EEA / UK Users)

  • Right of Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate personal data.
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Right to Restriction: Request that we restrict processing of your personal data.
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a portable format.
  • Right to Object: Object to our processing of your personal data.

CCPA (California Users)

  • Right to know what personal information we collect and how it is used.
  • Right to delete personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to opt out of the sale of your personal information. We do not sell personal information.
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at clavinovacs@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

Helm is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

8. Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect your information. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. We encourage you to use strong, unique API keys and to keep them confidential.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Your continued use of Helm after changes become effective constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
